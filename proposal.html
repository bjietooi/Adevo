01 The thesis

The bottom of the funnel is already built. Social's only job is to feed it.

adevo.sg now has what most competitors don't: an instant cost calculator, a free space-planning offer, a Singapore approvals guide, a real client on video (EU Holidays), and every path ending in WhatsApp. That is a finished conversion engine with almost nothing driving traffic into it.

The second thing to be clear-eyed about: ADEVO's buyer is a business owner, not a homeowner. The Instagram tactics that work for HDB-reno firms produce homeowner DMs. A commercial lead machine needs a dual track:

  • Volume track — Meta Click-to-WhatsApp ads. The exact funnel Singapore's reno giants run at scale, aimed instead at business-owner segments with commercial-only creative.
  • Authority track — founder-led LinkedIn + Google reviews + YouTube case studies. The channels where office, clinic and F&B decision-makers actually vet a contractor before they ever message one.

The economics make this easy to justify: one won fit-out project pays for the entire year of this plan many times over. The machine doesn't need to go viral — it needs to produce a handful of qualified WhatsApp conversations per week and never miss one.

03 What the winners actually do

Three playbooks, observed — and a gap ADEVO can own

Singapore's consumer-reno giants: always-on click-to-chat

Reading the Meta Ad Library directly: Weiken runs ~28 simultaneous ads, nearly all ending in a chat button, not a website form. Carpenters has kept a price-in-the-headline ad ("$100,000 Cream Minimalist Design… Kempas Rd") running for 4+ months — cost transparency is the copy theme that survives. Swiss Interior turned one celebrity client story into an ad that ran from March to July. The pattern: named projects, real prices, chat capture, and winning creatives left running for months.

Global design & build brands: content that compounds

  • Cost-transparency content is the documented king. River Pools (US) built ~$35M in tracked sales from answering "how much does it cost" in public. Every ADEVO analogue ("what a café fit-out really costs in SG") feeds the calculator ADEVO already built.
  • Founder authority compounds. Matt Risinger grew a build firm to ~$20M alongside a teach-everything YouTube channel; Oktra — the UK's leading office design & build firm — runs its portfolio as searchable long-form case studies. Shanon's architect-on-site story is the same genre.
  • Before/after short-form converts at tiny view counts (a documented remodeler example closed real projects off a 2,000-view reel), and comment-keyword DM automation ("comment PRICE") turns viewers into captured chats.

The gap

None of the Singapore commercial fit-out firms we checked run Meta ads at all (Conexus, Livspace SG, Ovon at time of audit). The consumer giants own homeowner attention; nobody is running WhatsApp-CTA ads aimed at the business owner planning an office, café or clinic. That's the white space this plan occupies.

06 Paid plan

Click-to-WhatsApp at the core — start small, scale on evidence

Meta Click-to-WhatsApp ads open a chat directly from the ad — no form, no landing-page drop-off — and plug straight into the funnel ADEVO already runs. For high-ticket, sales-closed services they consistently produce fewer but better leads than instant forms.

07 Tracking & compliance

Every lead carries its source; every message stays legal

The tracking spine

WhatsApp links can't carry analytics tags into the chat — so the pre-filled first message is the tracking code. The site already sends "[From website]". Extend the same convention per channel:

SourceFirst message
Website[From website]
Instagram / Facebook organic[From IG] · [From FB]
Meta ads[From ad]
LinkedIn[From LinkedIn]
Google profile[From Google]
  • Label the source on every new chat at first touch; the weekly scoreboard is then just counting labels: conversations → qualified → site visits → quotes → won, by source.
  • One tagged calculator URL as the only link-in-bio everywhere, with the Meta pixel on the page — enables retargeting site visitors even before ads start.

Compliance guardrails (Singapore)

  • No cold WhatsApp outreach. Unsolicited marketing messages to SG numbers fall under the Do-Not-Call provisions; this plan is 100% inbound — people message ADEVO first, which is clean opt-in.
  • PDPA basics: store the lead list responsibly, honour opt-outs, and get written consent for client testimonials, project photography, and any staff or visitors appearing in site footage.
  • Filming on live sites: landlord/MCST permission, pre-opening embargoes respected, PPE in every frame.

10 Scope discipline

What we're deliberately not doing (yet)

  • TikTok — viable in this vertical (a SG reno platform cut acquisition costs ~26% with lookalikes) but it's a volume/consumer channel; revisit once the Meta engine is profitable.
  • Qanvast / consumer marketplaces — they feed homeowner leads and skew residential; wrong ICP. Fix the empty HomeTrust shell, then deprioritise.
  • Discount offers — attract price-shoppers and undercut the architect-led premium positioning. Cost transparency, never cost cutting.
  • Malaysia targeting — phase 2, once SG numbers are proven; the WORQ KL project becomes the anchor case study when it starts.
  • Marketing automation platforms / WhatsApp API — the free WhatsApp Business app (labels, quick replies, catalog) is enough until lead volume genuinely outgrows it.

Main risk is capacity, not strategy: the cadence in §5 is sized for a small firm, and the weekly scoreboard exists precisely so the plan gets judged on qualified conversations — not on follower counts or posting streaks.
