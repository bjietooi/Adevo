Free Guide

Before you can open: the 5 approvals every Singapore fit-out must clear.

MCST · SCDF · SP Services · LEW · BCA — who submits what, and how long each takes. Miss one and you risk a stop-work order or no power on opening day. As a QS- and architecture-led firm, ADEVO sequences all five in parallel from day one, so approvals never become the reason your opening slips.

1

Building Management / MCST

Renovation application → Permit-to-Work, plus a refundable fit-out deposit. Required before works begin.

Who · Tenant/contractor — ADEVO lodges it for you

Timeline · ~5–10 working days

2

SCDF / FSSD — Fire Safety

Fire-safety plans lodged via CORENET by a Qualified Person; a Fire Safety Certificate is required before you occupy.

Who · Qualified Person (Architect / PE)

Timeline · Plan review ~3–4 weeks

3

SP Services — Utilities

Commercial utilities account plus electricity/water turn-on. A deposit is payable; commercial power-on is gated by SP testing and the LEW.

Who · Tenant opens account; LEW handles turn-on

Timeline · Account in minutes; activation ~1–3 days

4

LEW — Licensed Electrical Worker

Certifies the electrical installation; required above a 45 kVA approved load. Issues the Certificate of Compliance for turn-on.

Who · Owner appoints the correctly graded LEW

Timeline · Runs alongside the fit-out

5

BCA — Structural / A&A Works

A structural / A&A permit where load-bearing elements or mezzanines are involved. A PE prepares plans; a registered builder is required.

Who · PE + owner + builder (joint)

Timeline · ~2–3 weeks (complex ~4–6)
